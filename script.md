
  ## Website walkthrough script

  ### 0:00–0:12 — Landing page

  Start at the hero, then scroll toward the workflow animation.

  > “This is Max’s public dashboard. The landing page shows the complete
  > mission loop—from the original request, through marketplace search and
  > Prava approval, to delivery tracking and the robot handoff.”

  Pause briefly on the animated Ask → Shop → Approve → Bring it back sequence.

  ### 0:12–0:35 — Live Mission

  Open Live mission.

  > “The Live Mission page reads directly from our deployed backend. It
  > combines robot camera and location availability, the selected product,
  > exact quote, payment state, and every safe mission event.”

  Point at the event feed:

  > “Here, OpenAI understood the request, Swiggy returned the quote, Prava
  > created the approval session, and the owner approved that exact amount.
  > Addresses, credentials, and control access are intentionally excluded from
  > the public feed.”

  If the Pi can be online during recording, do that. Otherwise move past the
  waiting camera/GPS cards quickly.

  ### 0:35–0:52 — History

  Open History, then expand the declined mission.

  > “Every mission is persisted with its complete timeline. We can inspect the
  > commerce, payment, checkout, and fulfilment states individually.”

  Scroll through the expanded events.

  > “Failures are recorded too. A declined or cancelled payment stops the
  > workflow cleanly instead of being retried or presented as a successful
  > order.”

  This is especially useful Prava proof.

  ### 0:52–1:07 — ONDC

  Open ONDC sandbox, show the offer graphic, then scroll to the protocol
  workbench.

  > “The commerce layer is not restricted to one marketplace. Swiggy Instamart
  > is our live path, while ONDC demonstrates discovery across multiple sellers
  > through search, select, init, confirm, and status.”

  Point at the owner boundary:

  > “Whichever commerce path is used, it rejoins the same Prava approval and
  > mission record.”

  ### 1:07–1:15 — Transition to robot footage

  Return briefly to the Live page or the landing-page handoff section.

  > “When the order reaches campus, the same mission continues into the
  > physical handoff.”

  Cut immediately to your friend’s robot footage.
